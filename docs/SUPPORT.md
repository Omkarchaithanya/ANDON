# ANDON Support & Service Level Agreement (SUPPORT.md)

## Support Channel
- **Support Contact Email:** `support-andon@robotics-clearance.org` (Monitored engineering queue, non-personal address)
- **Issue Tracker:** Public GitHub repository issue tracker or Jira Service Management customer portal
- **Documentation & Architecture Guide:** Available in repository `/docs/SPEC.md` and `/docs/DESIGN.md`

---

## Operating Hours & Response SLA

| Priority Level | Classification | Support Hours | Initial Response Target | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 - Critical** | App failure blocking critical maintenance or causing false clearance in production robotized zones | 24/7 Monitoring | < 2 Hours | < 8 Hours |
| **P2 - High** | Feature impairment in Custom UI redline inspection or follow-up dispatch, with manual workaround available | Monday – Friday, 08:00 – 18:00 UTC | < 4 Hours | < 24 Hours |
| **P3 - Standard** | General technical inquiries, configuration assistance with Confluence Site Registry, or non-blocking issues | Monday – Friday, 08:00 – 18:00 UTC | < 8 Hours | < 3 Business Days |

---

## Escalation & Maintenance Windows
- Scheduled maintenance follows Atlassian Cloud release schedules.
- Version upgrades and schema migrations are verified using automated backward-compatibility tests before rollout.
